## Description

Masinloc Connect brings the Masinloc community closer in one mobile experience.

Browse local businesses in Marketplace, find current job opportunities from trusted providers, explore Mabayani history, search the Sambal Tina Dictionary, discover Masinloc stories and places, and send an incident report to the appropriate local response desk.

The app is designed as the action layer of the Masinloc Connect platform. Public community information remains connected to the same source used by the Masinloc Connect website, so the mobile experience does not maintain a separate copy of the town's public content.

Key features include:

- Marketplace discovery for local Masinloc businesses
- Current job opportunities and trusted-provider attribution
- Optional saved jobs and Signature Resume tools
- Mabayani documented history and source notes
- Searchable Sambal Tina Dictionary
- Discover Masinloc stories and local information
- Incident reporting with PNP or MDRRMO routing
- Optional GPS location for incident reports, with barangay or landmark entry as an alternative
- Offline-safe local incident saving until connectivity is available
- Optional passwordless email account for saved and personalized features

You do not need an account to browse public information or to send an incident report.

Masinloc Connect is an independent community platform and should not be represented as an official government application unless that status is formally established outside this release.

## App Review notes

- No account is required to browse public content, Marketplace listings, jobs, Mabayani, Sambal Tina, Discover content, or to submit an incident report.
- `Continue with Email` is optional and is used for saved/personalized features such as saved jobs, saved content, profile information and Signature Resume.
- Location permission is requested only in the incident-report flow when a user chooses to attach device location. A barangay or landmark can be entered instead.
- A report saved while offline is clearly marked as not yet received until the production emergency service accepts it.
- External job application buttons may open the trusted job provider's website.
- Account deletion is available from Profile → Delete account and from the public account-deletion URL above.
- Push notification UI is not advertised in this release. Native push capability is packaged for later activation after APNs/FCM credentials and notification behavior are fully configured.
